How MeterX measures

A full test takes about twenty seconds and runs in four phases. Each one answers a different question about your line.

Where the twenty seconds go

Each bar shows the phase's share of the whole test.

  • Under a second

    Connect

    MeterX opens a connection to the nearest Cloudflare location, or to your own server.

  • 2 seconds

    Ping and jitter

    A run of small requests measures the delay and how much it wobbles from one request to the next. Requests that never return count as loss.

  • About 9 seconds

    Download

    Four parallel streams fill your line for a fixed time. The slow start and the last moments are trimmed, so the figure is the steady speed.

  • About 9 seconds

    Upload

    Three streams do the same upward while pings keep running, to catch the delay that builds under load.

Latency under load

A line can look fast and still stutter on a call while something downloads. That is bufferbloat: routers queue too much data, so every request waits in line.

MeterX keeps pinging during the download and upload and grades the worst rise above the ping at rest.

How the load grade is set
GradeRise in ping under loadWhat you notice
A5 ms or lessNothing, even on calls during big downloads

FMore than 200 msEverything lags when the line is busy

Where it tests

  • The nearest Cloudflare locationBy default, a small Cloudflare Worker answers from the location closest to you. It sends and receives test bytes and keeps nothing.
  • Or your own serverRun the self-hosted server from the repository on your machine or network, and point MeterX at it in settings.
  • Why results differ from other testsEach test uses its own servers, number of streams and length. MeterX uses several streams for a fixed time, which is close to how your browser loads pages and files.
  • What a quality label meansExcellent needs 50 Mbps down, 20 up and under 30 ms ping. Good needs 20, 10 and under 60 ms. Fair needs 5, 2 and under 150 ms.
