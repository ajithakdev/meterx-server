# MeterX privacy policy

The current policy is published at [meterx.ajithakdev.com/privacy](https://meterx.ajithakdev.com/privacy/). Its source is [apps/site/static/privacy/index.html](apps/site/static/privacy/index.html), and that file's history is the policy's change log.

In short: MeterX collects no personal data. Results and settings stay in your browser's extension storage, the default test server keeps no logs, and nothing is sent anywhere except the test traffic itself.
